### 1. Who we are (Data Controller)

Launchino is operated by **Shexon BV**, a company registered in the Netherlands. Shexon BV is the data controller for the personal data described in this policy.

Contact for privacy matters: info@shexon.com

### 2. What personal data we collect

**If you're a candidate:**
- Account data: name, email address, password (stored only as a secure hash — we never store your actual password)
- Career profile data you provide through our survey: skills, work experience, task history, teamwork preferences, career direction, motivations, work-environment preferences, and practical details (such as location, availability, language proficiency, work-mode preferences, and whether you require visa sponsorship)
- CV text, if you choose to use AI-assisted CV extraction
- Subscription status for the paid Job Discovery feature, and whether you opted into preliminary match notifications
- Account activity: registration date, login timestamps, consent timestamp

**If you're a company:**
- Account data: company name, contact email, password (hashed)
- Vacancy information you submit: job descriptions, role requirements, and structured vacancy details
- Account activity: registration date, login timestamps, consent timestamp

**Automatically, for all users:**
- Basic technical/operational logs needed to run and secure the service (e.g., rate-limiting counters keyed to IP address, error logs, and AI-usage records for cost accounting)

We deliberately collect no more than the service needs. We do not collect special-category data (such as health, religious, or political information), and we ask you not to include such information in free-text fields or CVs.

### 4. AI-assisted processing — what AI does and doesn't do here

Some features use AI (Anthropic's Claude models) to assist with:
- **Extracting structured information** from text you provide (your CV, or a vacancy description) into your profile — **you always see and can edit the extracted results before anything is saved**
- **Generating plain-language explanations** of match results

What AI does **not** do:
- Match scores are calculated by **deterministic, rule-based logic** — not by an AI model making a judgment about you
- **No solely automated decision-making with legal or similarly significant effect** (GDPR Art. 22) takes place: no candidate is automatically rejected, filtered out, or ranked down by an AI decision. Where information is missing or unclear, the system explicitly marks it as "unknown" or "needs clarification" rather than guessing, and human review is built into the workflow.

### 5. Who your data is shared with

**Registered companies on the platform:** the core purpose of Launchino is making candidate profiles discoverable to companies with relevant vacancies. Companies see the profile information relevant to evaluating fit. Companies are required under our Terms to use candidate data only for their own recruitment purposes.

**Service providers (processors) that host and power the platform:**
- **Anthropic** (USA) — AI processing of text you submit for extraction, and generation of match explanations
- **Render** (backend hosting), **Vercel** (frontend hosting), **Neon** (database hosting) — infrastructure; regions: west EU

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers.

### 7. How long we keep your data

- **Active accounts:** for as long as your account exists
- **Inactive accounts:** flagged after 12 months of inactivity; anonymized after 18 months if you don't respond to a reactivation notice
- **After a deletion request:** your identifying data is anonymized and your submitted free-text content is deleted promptly; residual copies are purged from backups within 30 days
- **Anonymized match records:** where a company evaluated your profile against a vacancy, an anonymized record of that evaluation may be retained as part of the company's own hiring records — it can no longer be linked to you
- **Operational logs** (AI cost records, security logs): up to 12 months, then purged

### 8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:
- **Access** your personal data (you can request a full export)
- **Rectification** — correct inaccurate data (you can edit your own profile at any time)
- **Erasure** — request deletion of your account and personal data
- **Restriction** of processing in certain circumstances
- **Data portability** — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format (our export provides this)
- **Object** to processing based on legitimate interest
- **Withdraw consent** at any time, where processing is based on consent, without affecting prior processing

To exercise any of these rights, contact info@shexon.com or use the export/deletion functions in your account. We will respond within one month.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Dutch supervisory authority: **Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens** (autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl), or the supervisory authority of your own EU member state.

### 9. Security

We protect your data with, among other measures: password hashing (bcrypt), encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) throughout, role-based access control (candidates, companies, and administrators can each only access what they're entitled to), rate limiting against abuse, and input validation on all public endpoints.

### 10. Age requirement

Launchino is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

### 11. Cookies and local storage

Launchino uses minimal browser storage: an authentication token kept in your browser so you stay logged in. We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

We may update this policy as the product evolves. Material changes will be communicated to active users by email or in-app notice before they take effect.
