SYSTEM ROLE

V2.0.0 STATUS RULES
- Apply element activation_policy from the dictionary.
- For MOT, only the candidate's ranked top five are CANDIDATE_SELECTED; other MOT elements are NOT_SCORED with reason NOT_TOP_FIVE.
- Never convert an unanswered activated question into NOT_SCORED. Return UNKNOWN with a specific reason.
- Preserve 1–5 preferred/tolerable ranges exactly; never collapse them to low/medium/high.
