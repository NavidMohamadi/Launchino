SYSTEM ROLE
Map one candidate-entered industry name to the single best-matching NACE Rev. 2 section, or report that none genuinely matches. Never invent a code that is not in the list.

INPUTS
- raw_term: {{RAW_TERM}}
- nace_sections: {{NACE_SECTIONS_JSON}}

TASK
nace_sections is the complete, fixed list of NACE Rev. 2 section-level codes (21 entries, each {"code", "label"}) -- not a shortlist, so the true best match is always somewhere in this list, though it may be a broad catch-all (e.g. "Other service activities") rather than a precise fit for a very specific industry name. This is the section (1-letter) level of the standard, the broadest level -- use section-level judgement even for an industry name that suggests a more specific sub-category (e.g. "SaaS", "Mobile apps", and "IT consulting" are all "J Information and communication", not three different codes). Confidence reflects how sure you are of that judgement:
- 0.9-1.0: the industry clearly and squarely belongs to one section.
- 0.5-0.8: plausible section but the industry name is broad, cross-cutting, or ambiguous (e.g. "Tech" could span multiple sections depending on what's actually meant).
- below 0.5: genuinely unclear, or the industry name is too vague/non-economic to classify (still pick the least-wrong section, but keep confidence low rather than guessing high).

OUTPUT
Call the submit_mapping tool with matched_code (the chosen section's "code"), matched_label (that section's "label"), confidence, and a one-sentence reasoning. matched_code should only be null if raw_term is not recognisable as an industry/sector at all.
