- candidate_profile: {{CANDIDATE_PROFILE_JSON}}
- role_profile: {{ROLE_PROFILE_JSON}}

CHECK
1. ALWAYS elements are active. This includes every CAP (skills) and TASK (work experience) element -- candidate-side profile content, always answerable independent of any vacancy, same as Practical fit/Career/Environment.

6. Inactive elements are NOT_SCORED with a reason and do not reduce coverage.

8. Selected teamwork behaviours are reused for every candidate in the vacancy.
9. No five-point value has been collapsed to low/medium/high.
